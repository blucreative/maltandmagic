## Corrections and distinctions

- Removed the duplicated expansion-class cards from Heritage. Core and expansion
  heritage remain together, labeled by source, with the old URL preserved.
- Added the one-transformation limit and distinguished optional story changes
  from automatic tier rewards. Creation bonuses persist but are applied once.
- Distinguished fixed damage dice, Proficiency dice, Spellcast dice, flat bonuses,
  and tier scaling. Resistance is resolved before thresholds and Armor Slots.
- Armor Score describes total slots, not remaining unmarked slots; its cap is 12.
- Domain card levels are not tiers. Higher levels do not automatically increase
  the five-card loadout; specific feature exceptions still apply.
- Subclass upgrades require advancement choices; they are not automatic awards
  at fixed character levels. Class charts still control available slots.
- Corrected Core frame citations from the previously listed PDF p. 186 to each
  actual frame title page (255, 263, 271, 281, 291, 309). Expansion citations now
  point to individual frame titles (117, 129, 145, 159).
- Campaign complexity is not character tier. Continuing-play prompts are original
  guidance, not claimed published level schedules or replacement frame mechanics.

## Boundaries

This is a read-only player reference, not a complete digital rulebook or rules
engine. It does not reproduce all campaign mechanics, GM material, illustrations,
or character advancement charts. Use the chosen frame and class chart for those
details. A listed option is not automatic campaign approval. No browser saves
are read, changed, or deleted. The private source corpus and build scripts remain
excluded from the Pages artifact.

Generated references retain source feature text; concise rules guidance and
heritage descriptions are editorial summaries. Tests verify transcription and
coverage, not the correctness of every ambiguity in the original conversion.
Source-dependent tests explicitly skip when their private source is absent.
