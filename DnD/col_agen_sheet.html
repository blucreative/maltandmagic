Sorcerer Features

Innate Sorcery

As a Bonus Action, unleash your magic for 1 minute. Your Sorcerer spell save DC increases to 14, and Sorcerer spell attacks have Advantage. Two uses per Long Rest.

Font of Magic

You have 2 Sorcery Points, restored on a Long Rest. You can expend a spell slot to gain Sorcery Points equal to its level, or use a Bonus Action and spend 2 Sorcery Points to create a level 1 spell slot. A created slot vanishes on a Long Rest.

Careful Spell

When a spell forces other creatures to save, spend 1 Sorcery Point and protect up to 3 of them. Each automatically succeeds and takes no damage if a successful save would normally deal half damage.

Quickened Spell

Spend 2 Sorcery Points to change a spell's casting time from an Action to a Bonus Action. You can't use this after casting a level 1+ spell that turn, or cast another level 1+ spell afterward.

Spellcasting

Charisma is your spellcasting ability. Your crystal is an Arcane Focus. Spell slots return on a Long Rest.

Tough

Your Hit Point maximum increases by 2 for every character level.

Halfling Traits

Luck

When you roll a 1 on the d20 of a D20 Test, reroll it and use the new roll.

Brave

Advantage on saves to avoid or end Frightened.

Nimble & Stealthy

Move through spaces of larger creatures. You can Hide while obscured only by a creature at least one size larger.
