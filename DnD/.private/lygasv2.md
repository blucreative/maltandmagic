## Attacks

| Weapon | Hit | Damage | Properties |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Judgement** (scimitar) | +6 | 1d6+4 slashing | Martial, Dual Wield, Finesse, Light, Nick |
| **Luck** (scimitar) | +6 | 1d6+4 slashing | Martial, Finesse, Light, Nick |
| Unarmed Strike | +2 | 1 bludgeoning | — |

Both scimitars use **Charisma** for attack and damage via Pact of the Blade's bond, hence +6 to hit and +4 damage rather than DEX-based numbers.

### Weapon Mastery
- **Nick (Scimitar):** the extra Light-property attack can be folded into the Attack action instead of costing a Bonus Action. Once per turn only.
- **Vex (Handaxe, Pistol):** a damaging hit grants advantage on the next attack roll against that target, until the end of his following turn.

### Damage Note
With Two-Weapon Fighting as his Fighting Style, the ability modifier is added to the off-hand attack's damage — which is why both scimitars read 1d6+4 rather than the off-hand dropping to 1d6.

## Actions

**Standard:** Attack, Magic, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, Utilize, Opportunity Attack, Grapple, Shove, Improvise, Two-Weapon Fighting, Interact with an Object, Study, Influence

**Bonus Action — Pact of the Blade: Conjure:** conjure a pact weapon into his hand.

**Bonus Action — Pact of the Blade: Bond:** bond with a magic weapon on touch. Can't bond with a weapon already attuned to someone else or bonded to another Warlock. While bonded he's proficient with it and can use it as a Spellcasting Focus. The bond ends if he uses the Bonus Action again, if the weapon is more than 5 ft. away for over a minute, or on his death. A conjured weapon vanishes when the bond ends.

**Bonus Action — Second Wind** (2 / Long Rest): regain 1d10+1 HP. One expended use returns on a Short Rest.

## GM Notes / Open Items

- **Subclasses not yet chosen** for either class. Warlock patron arrives at Warlock 3; Fighter subclass at Fighter 3.
- **Zero coin** on a Charlatan with a forgery kit and a costume is a hook in itself — he needs money and has the tools to get it dishonestly.
- **Named weapons.** Luck and Judgement are the strongest character detail on an otherwise blank sheet. Worth asking Steve where the names came from, and whether the pact weapon bond is on one of them or something else entirely.
- **The pact is undefined.** Pact of the Blade at Warlock 1 with no patron chosen means the *source* of his power is a completely open question — potentially useful given Tephra Lethe's interest in things that grant power.
- **Face of the party.** +6 in Deception, Intimidation, and Persuasion with a DC 14 Charm Person. Expect him to talk first.
- **Fragile for a front-liner.** 19 HP, AC 15, dual-wielding light weapons in melee. He fights like a rogue but doesn't have Cunning Action to get out.
