## Core Features

**Arcane Archer Lore (3)** — Druidcraft *or* Prestidigitation cantrip (INT-based). Proficiency in Arcana and Nature; substitute other skills for any already held.

**Arcane Shot (3)** — Once per turn, apply one known option to a ranged attack made with an Ammunition-property weapon. The choice is made after the hit lands and damage is dealt, except for options that skip the attack roll entirely.

**Curving Shot (7)** — On a miss with an Ammunition weapon, use a Bonus Action to attack a different visible target within long range that isn't behind Total Cover. Consumes no ammunition.

**Ever Ready Shot (7)** — Rolling Initiative with zero uses left restores one.

## Build Notes

- Intelligence drives everything — DC, number of uses, and the bonus cantrip. A dump-stat INT makes the subclass functionally inert, so a fighter taking this needs INT as a genuine secondary stat.
- Short-rest recharge means uses can be spent freely in a single fight rather than hoarded across the day.
- Piercing Shot and Seeking Shot bypass the attack roll entirely — useful against high-AC targets or foes behind cover.
- Ever Ready Shot guarantees at least one charge entering every combat regardless of how the last one ended.
- Playtest caveat worth raising with any player who wants this: the numbers may shift, and it isn't legal for organised play.
