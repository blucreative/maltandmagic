### Character Backstory

I was born in raised in Greywood and groomed and trained from birth as part of the Foresters like my mother and father and their mother and father before them. It is tradition to only have 1 child per family. People do have more than one child as they are allowed to. Its just easier to focus solely on one child to train them and get them to live up to their potential. I am an only child. Its been a generational tradition in Greywood to train and be hunters and protectors but the reasons why have become more myth and legend and our decrees and oaths are more ritualistic and of a bygone era. Over time what ever we originally started out as hundreds of years ago has evolved to become what is now known as The Foresters. The aim was always to become a member of the monster hunting factions So I can protect from a greater sphere of influence outside of The Foresters and my immediate home. I believe that the bonds of brotherhood and team can be as strong as blood
