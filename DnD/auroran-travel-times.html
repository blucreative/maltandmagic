Roads of Aurora

Expected journey times between every city of the Empire, as measured by a sensible traveller with sound boots, a serviceable cart, and no urgent reason to arrive early.

The Imperial Standard

The figures below assume eight hours of travel each day, established roads where they exist, ordinary weather, and nightly stops. They include routine ferries, toll gates, meals, and the small delays that turn a distance on a map into a journey through the Empire.

On FootUse the listed time
MountedReduce by roughly one third

A Necessary Caution

These are planning estimates, not promises. Fen floods, mountain closures, military traffic, contrary winds, and an especially thorough magistrate can each add a day. Beyond Thornwall, the Imperial reckoning ends.

The Great Routes

Fen Road · Heartfen

Shimmerfen to Hollowhearth

The best-maintained road in the north runs south from the capital on raised stone, passing Reedmere before turning toward the Codex Halls. Floodwater may cover the fen, but it rarely covers the road.

3 days · 2 days mounted
Imperial Road · Pass Crossing

Shimmerfen to Vareth

The eastern road passes Dunmore, climbs to Ironpass, and crosses the Pass of Dernavar before descending toward the Varethi coast. The pass is the journey: in winter, its condition decides the timetable.

11 days · add 2–4 days in winter
North–South Artery · Grandbelt

Shimmerfen to Cindral

Travellers leave the fen through Ferngate, skirt the Aldwood, and emerge onto the open grain roads near Millhaven. South of the forest the road is broad, exposed, and busy with military and harvest traffic.

8 days · 5 days by relay post
The Maren · River Passage

Cindral to Marenfall

Grain barges follow the Maren west through Tolmen to the Ocean of Conflux. Passage downstream is quick and plentiful; the return depends on towpaths, sail, and the river's seasonal temper.

4 days downstream · 6 days upriver
Southern Road · Frontier

Cindral to Thornwall

The road narrows beyond Stockend, where cultivated plains give way to the Ashfringe. Waymark and Ashford divide the long southern run into manageable stages before the volcanic ridge fills the horizon.

7 days · reliable outside ash storms
Ocean of Ophidra · Sea Passage

Vareth to Velara

Regular packets sail south along the Varethi coast before crossing to Corsala. A direct captain can make the passage swiftly; most scheduled ships call at one coastal harbour before reaching Velara.

4 days · 3 with favourable winds

Seasonal Reckoning

SpringFen floods and swollen rivers add one day to northern road journeys.
SummerFast roads, low rivers, and punishing Grandbelt heat. Start before dawn.
AutumnHarvest traffic adds a day around Cindral, Tolmen, and the main grain roads.
WinterThe Pass of Dernavar may add two to four days or close without notice.
