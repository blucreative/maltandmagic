An Imperial Wayfarer's Reckoning

Roads of Aurora

Map-scaled journey estimates between every city of the Empire, calculated for travellers moving at a normal overland pace.

How Distances Are Reckoned

Each estimate measures the direct map distance, then adds a 15% route factor for bends, roads, river crossings, and local geography. Normal travel covers 24 miles each day; round the result in play rather than treating it as a survey.

Normal · 24 mi/dayAll listed day counts
Fast · 30 mi/daySuitable terrain and good roads
Sea Crossings

Routes to Corsala or Whitecliff cross open water. Their mileage is useful for scale, but the overland day count does not apply. Passage time depends on the vessel, weather, and departure schedule.
