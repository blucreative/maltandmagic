Core Subclass Features
Arcane Archer Lore Level 3
Learn Druidcraft or Prestidigitation (INT). Gain proficiency in Arcana and Nature.
Arcane Shot Level 3
Once per turn, apply a known option to a ranged attack with an Ammunition weapon — chosen after the hit and damage (unless it skips the attack roll).
Curving Shot Level 7
On a miss, Bonus Action to attack a different visible target in long range not behind Total Cover. No ammunition used.
Ever Ready Shot Level 7
Rolling Initiative with zero Arcane Shot uses left restores one.
Progression: L3 → 2 options · L7 → 3 (+Curving/Ever Ready) · L10 → 4 (die d8) · L15 → 5 (d10) · L18 → 6 (d12). Swap one known option each time you gain a new one.

Character Backstory
Backstory unrecorded on the export — open hooks await. A Wayfarer with thieves' tools and a set of dice suggests a rootless, streetwise past. His wide, even spread of 17s in Dexterity, Intelligence, and Wisdom paints a scout or outrider — capable, observant, and quick-thinking rather than a front-line bruiser, and a natural fit for the Arcane Archer's blend of bowcraft and low magic.
