The advancement catalog restructures SRD class tables, feature text, spell
lists, and spell descriptions for interactive use. Markdown input is supplied
by https://github.com/downfallx/dnd-5e-srd-markdown.

Supplemental subclass names, progression data, and recorded campaign choices
are separately labeled non-SRD. They are not covered by the SRD license.
Private supplemental prose is not included in the generated catalog.
Arcane Archer is Unearthed Arcana 2025 playtest material, not final rules.
